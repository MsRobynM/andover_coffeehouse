The Andover Community
Coffeehouse & Open Mic.

A new musical venue in the Town of Andover, NH. featuring folk and acoustic music

Join us on February 20TH at 7:00PM for our next concert.

Our featured performer will be Kathy Lowe Bloch

 

Kathy Lowe Bloch

.Kathy Lowe Bloch is a multi-talented, multi-faceted singer/songwriter and artist. Music for adults, kids, and everyone else (you know who you are!)

From a lineage of circus/vaudeville folks, Kathy shares her retrospective of songwriting from heartfelt depth and humor.

With guitar, dulcimer, and drum, her voice invites us in.

 

At Highland Lake Grange Hall on the corner of Rte 11 & Chase Hill Road, East Andover, NH. Click here for a Map and Directions

Doors open at 6:30PM for Refreshments and open mic sign-ups

Admission is free

More to come on the 3rd Friday of every month. Come to play or come to listen. Come for a light dinner or a snack. We are a listening room environment. Most of all we are here for the community, so come and be welcome.
